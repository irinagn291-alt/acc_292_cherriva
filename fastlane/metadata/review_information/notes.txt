The app never asks for camera, photos, microphone, location, tracking, or notifications. No permission prompt appears, and there is no usage-description string.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. You save catalog works and marks; you do not post or create public content.

Cherriva is a Courtauld painting quiz for people who keep works on this device. You save a painting, the app writes one word twice on the maker line or the title line, and you tap that twin then Elide to file the work as fair. Misses stay on Saved as botches you can undo.

No credentials or demo account are needed. On a new install the system launch screen appears, then a blank pause that can last several seconds. Nothing is tappable during that pause; it continues on its own. The first pages then cover the app. Each page has illustration, a title, a line of copy, page dots, a full-width bottom button, and “Skip”. Title “Save a painting”. Line “Keep Courtauld works on this device, then sit the quiz.” Button “Next”. Title “Find the twin”. Line “Echo writes one word twice. Tap either neighbor to elide.” Button “Next”. Title “File it fair”. Line “A true elide files the work. Botches stay reviewable on Saved.” Button “Continue”. “Skip” on any page, or “Continue” on the last page, dismisses the cover and opens Home. A first session on a device can also show a banner “Started a new echo.” with “Try again” across the top of Home. Tap “Try again” and the banner leaves. It is not an error you must fix to use the app.

There is no tab bar. Home stays underneath. Explore, Saved, Settings, and The twin open as sheets with “Close” in the bar. From Home, tap the Explore icon button (no visible caption; VoiceOver “Explore”). Leave “Maker or title” empty so the local shelf stays. Tap “A Bar at the Folies Bergere”, “Edouard Manet”, to save that work into your crate. A “Saved” tag appears. Tap “Close”. If Home shows “The line is plain.” with “Save a work, then elide.”, save a work, close the sheet, then tap “Write twice”. When status is “Echoed”, the twin pair is already highlighted; tap “Elide” to file it fair. After a fair file, tap “Write twice” for another saved work that is not yet fair. Open “Saved” for “Fair” and “Botches”. Open “How the twin works” for The twin. In Settings, use “The Courtauld”, “Collection”, “Wikidata Q1138087”, “Undo newest mark”, “Contact us”, “How the twin works”, “Show the first pages”, and “Reset all data” with “Keep my crate” to cancel. If “Restored the last good echo.” appears, tap “Try again”. On Simulator only, a demo crate can already hold several of those works, a live doubled line, fair files, botches, and skipped first pages. Paintings can sit as a blank muted tile until the picture arrives. A miss cools that word; highlight either matching neighbor, then tap “Elide”.

Typing in Explore looks up The Courtauld catalog; a miss still leaves the local shelf. Saved paintings, the live caption, and every mark stay on this device. The support control is “Contact us” under Help in Settings; it opens the support page.

English UI only; no in-app language or region switch. Elide and botch counts follow the device’s number grouping. Day numbers on Saved are eight-digit calendar dates for the device time zone. Search is by maker or title against English catalog titles. Portrait only on iPhone and iPad, full screen, Light appearance. Minimum iOS 17.0. On iPad with iOS 18, Settings uses a large page sheet so the reset sentence stays on screen.
